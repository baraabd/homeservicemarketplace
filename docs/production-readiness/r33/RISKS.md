## Security and privacy boundaries

Financial reads require existing explicit grants. R33 adds no permission seed/grant and does not upgrade ordinary administrators or providers. Test grants belong only to newly created synthetic accounts in disposable databases. Provider details are owner-scoped and missing/foreign records are indistinguishable.

Exports are bounded, authorized and formula-protected. Only financial identifiers, amount semantics and constrained public reason codes are eligible. Internal notes, restricted receipt pointers/bytes, identity evidence, emails and account/provider payloads stay excluded. A revoked grant cannot authorize a subsequent export request. Client cancellation prevents a response from an old actor becoming a new actor's download.

No live rail, payout, escrow, fee/tax rule, pricing decision, production verification change, account authorization or external paid resource is enabled by this sprint. The test scanner remains explicitly non-production and proves no malware-scanning acceptance.

## Operational limits

The read model is deliberately page-bounded. CSV exports one selected page, not an implicit complete report. UI totals cannot be treated as available funds, payable profit or a guarantee of external settlement. Existing booking reports retain their older separate purpose and R18 wording.

The offline JSON verifier proves wire consistency only. It cannot authenticate a document, prove a ledger posting happened or elevate an unavailable reconciliation into a successful run. Any required final-head test that is missing, failed, cancelled or skipped remains unaccepted.
