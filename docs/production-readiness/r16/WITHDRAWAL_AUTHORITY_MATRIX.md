## Consequences for R16

1. There is no funded balance to withdraw. Exposing `availableBalance` or a
   ledger net position as spendable would convert estimates or unfunded
   postings into money — forbidden (R15 no-backfill boundary).
2. The `availableBalance` contract comment ("funds the provider could withdraw
   today") overstates authority. Correcting its meaning belongs to the owner's
   funding decision; R16 records it and does not relabel the UI on its own.
3. The bid/booking unit ambiguity (whole vs. minor units) and mixed-currency
   sums would propagate into any payout amount. They must be resolved with an
   approved data interpretation before any booking-derived figure feeds the
   ledger — not by rescaling history.
4. The disabled CTA is the correct current state and is now guarded against
   both enablement and any write request.
